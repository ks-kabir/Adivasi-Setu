The Challenge

Why Tribal Students Miss Out on Scholarship Grants

Over 40% of eligible tribal students fail to receive allocated welfare funds due to administrative fragmentation and complex verification hurdles.

🌐

Scattered Information

Scholarship notifications are scattered across state portals, central sites, and departmental notices with conflicting deadlines.

❓

Eligibility Confusion

Students cannot easily tell if their family income ceiling, course level, or domicile matches complex official eligibility rules.

📑

Repeated Documentation

ST certificates, income proofs, and marksheets must be repeatedly scanned, sized, and resubmitted for every single scheme.

⏱️

Difficult Tracking

After submitting, students have zero visibility into where their file is stuck, whether documents were rejected, or when DBT will arrive.

About AdivasiSetu

Bridging Educational Opportunity for India's Tribal Youth

AdivasiSetu ("Bridge for Tribal Welfare") is built to ensure that no Scheduled Tribe student is deprived of higher education due to financial hardship or information asymmetry. By unifying the Ministry of Tribal Affairs’ five primary schemes into a single digital ecosystem, AdivasiSetu provides transparent access, automated rule checking, and real-time application tracking.

Our Mission Statement:

"To democratize scholarship access for all 705+ notified Scheduled Tribe communities across India by transforming a fragmented bureaucratic maze into a dignified, explainable, and accessible digital journey."

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AdivasiSetu?

AdivasiSetu is an integrated scholarship portal designed specifically for Scheduled Tribe students, providing one-stop access to Pre-Matric, Post-Matric, Top Class, NFST, and National Overseas schemes.

How does the eligibility matching engine work?

Our rule engine checks 6 dimensions: Category (20%), Academic Level (20%), Family Income (20%), Qualifying Marks (15%), State Domicile (10%), and Document Readiness (15%) to yield an explainable match score.

What documents are required for application?

Standard mandatory documents include Aadhaar Card, ST Community Certificate, Family Income Certificate, Previous Exam Marksheet, Aadhaar-linked Bank Passbook, and Admission/Bonafide Certificate.
