🔄 Official 5-Step Application Process

  • 1
    Profile & Identity Verification: Ensure your Aadhaar, tribal community certificate, and annual family income certificate are registered.
  • 2
    Online Submission: Click "Apply for Scholarship" to generate your permanent application tracking ID.
  • 3
    Desk Scrutiny: The state tribal welfare directorate verifies student bonafide and revenue documents.
  • 4
    Committee Approval: Sanction orders are generated by the Welfare Board according to scheme norms.
  • 5
    Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT): Sanctioned grant funds are credited directly to your Aadhaar-seeded bank account.
